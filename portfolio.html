Common Questions

What types of weddings are in your portfolio?

The portfolio showcases ceremonies, receptions, portrait sessions, and detail shots from weddings across Victoria, Vancouver, and British Columbia.

Can I view full-resolution versions of photos?

Yes! Clicking any photo in the portfolio opens it in full resolution. Contact James for access to the full gallery from your event.

Do you shoot both indoor and outdoor weddings?

Yes, James has experience with all types of wedding venues, from outdoor garden ceremonies to indoor ballroom receptions.
