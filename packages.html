FAQ

Common Questions

Do you travel outside British Columbia?
Absolutely. Destination weddings are some of my favourite to shoot. Travel fees apply based on location, and I'm happy to provide a custom quote for any destination.
How long until we receive our photos?
You'll receive a sneak peek within 48 hours of your wedding (a few favourite images to share!). Your full gallery will be delivered within 2 weeks via a private online gallery.
Do we get the digital files?
Yes, all packages include a private online gallery with high-resolution digital downloads. You have a print release so you can print anywhere you'd like.
How do we book?
A 50% deposit secures your date, with the balance due 30 days before your wedding day. Once you're ready, simply reach out via the contact page and I'll send over a contract.
What if something comes up and we need to reschedule?
Life happens. If you need to move your date, your deposit transfers to your new date as long as it's available. No stress, no penalties.
