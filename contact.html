Common Questions

How do I contact James X?

Fill out the contact form on this page or call/text 778-990-5608. All enquiries are responded to promptly.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Please include your name, email, how you found James X, and details about your wedding day including date, venue, and style.

How far in advance should I book?

Peak wedding season books 6-12 months in advance. Off-season dates may be available with shorter notice.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes! James travels throughout British Columbia for wedding photography. Travel fees may apply for locations outside Victoria and Vancouver.
