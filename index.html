Common Questions

Who is James X?

James X is a professional wedding photographer based in Vancouver, serving Victoria and all of Vancouver Island. Formerly a landscape photographer, he now captures authentic love stories with a cinematic, romantic style.

What areas do you serve?

James X Wedding Photography serves Victoria, Vancouver, and all of British Columbia. Destination weddings across BC are welcome.

How much does wedding photography cost?

Packages range from $1,800 for a Half Day to $2,800 for Full Day coverage. Custom packages available upon request.

What is your photography style?

James specializes in cinematic, romantic, and timeless wedding photography. His approach focuses on capturing authentic emotions and candid moments.

How do I book?

Contact James through the contact form or call directly at 778-990-5608. All enquiries receive a prompt response.
